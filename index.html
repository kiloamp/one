Regulation

Mandatory baseline

Regulation (EU) 2026/781 applies from 30 Apr 2028, with selected TRI provisions applying from 30 Apr 2026. ORA.ATO.135(b)(1) requires the ATO to demonstrate adequacy of FSTD specifications for the training programme using the FSTD qualification certificate and ESL.

AMC / GM

Optional method, controlled evidence

EASA describes task-to-tool as optional for aeroplane and helicopter type rating training. When an ATO adopts it, the evidence should show task objective, required capability and fidelity, device capability, differences, mitigations, and credit.

CS-FSTD

Device capability language

CS-FSTD Issue 1, issued by ED Decision 2026/008/R, supplies the qualification framework for FSTDs with FCS. The ATO uses the certificate and ESL as source evidence; it does not re-qualify the device.

Guidance

Implementation programme

EASA's implementation material points to IST.0007, a familiarisation session on 15 Dec 2026, workshops in 2027, FAQs, and a planned implementation support manual in Q1 2027.

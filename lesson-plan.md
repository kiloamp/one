## FFS 1: SOP Introduction, Basic ECAM & ILS

- Detail: Conversion Detail 1
- Route: LEBL → LEPA

### Briefing

- Session focus: SOP introduction, basic ECAM handling and repeated ILS profiles.
- Crew sequence: CM1 first, followed by reposition and CM2.
- Approach exercises: Decelerated AP approach, stabilised manual-thrust approach and No Flight Director ILS.
- Training emphasis: Normal SOP discipline, task sharing, holding procedures, go-around and normal landing.

### Aircraft Setup

- Aircraft state: Cold aircraft
- Performance: Take-off performance calculation required
- ZFW: 61.8 t
- ZFWCG: 29.4%
- FOB: 7.2 t
- METAR: LEBL 070520Z 09008KT 9999 FEW025 24/17 Q1016 NOSIG
- Departure: LEBL
- Arrival: LEPA

### Lesson Items

1. [Ground] Aircraft setup from cold and performance calculation.
2. [Taxi] Engine start and taxi out — CM1.
3. [Take Off] Normal take-off.
4. [Climb/Cruise] SID – Climb – Cruise – Descent – STAR – Hold.
5. [Climb/Cruise] In hold: Left Tank Fuel Pump Low Pressure — ECAM.
6. [Approach] Radar vectors to ILS approach and go-around — decelerated approach with autopilot.
7. [Approach] Radar vectors to ILS approach and go-around — stabilised approach with manual thrust.
8. [Approach] Radar vectors to ILS approach with normal landing — No Flight Director.
9. [Rollout] Vacate runway and taxi to stand.
10. [Ground] Shutdown checks.
11. [Taxi] Reposition/start-up and taxi out — CM2. (reposition)
12. [Take Off] Take-off and climb FL100.
13. [Climb/Cruise] Descent – STAR – Holding Pattern.
14. [Approach] Radar vectors to ILS and go-around — decelerated approach with autopilot.
15. [Approach] Radar vectors to ILS and go-around — stabilised approach with manual thrust.
16. [Approach] Radar vectors to ILS approach with normal landing — No Flight Director.
17. [Rollout] Vacate runway and taxi to stand.
18. [Ground] Full shutdown.

## FFS 2: Flight Control Laws, UPRT, TCAS & NPA

- Detail: Conversion Detail 2
- Route: LEBL → LEPA

### Briefing

- Session focus: Flight-control protections, Alternate Law, UPRT, TCAS and procedural VOR approaches.
- Crew sequence: CM1 exercise block followed by CM2 repeat.
- UPRT: Normal Law protections, Alternate Law stability and nose-high/nose-low recovery.
- Approach: Procedural VOR approach with autopilot and crosswind landing.

### Aircraft Setup

- Aircraft state: Cold aircraft
- Performance: Normal performance calculation
- ZFW: 62.6 t
- ZFWCG: 28.8%
- FOB: 8.1 t
- METAR: LEBL 080550Z 06014KT 9999 SCT030 23/16 Q1018
- Training altitude: FL200

### Lesson Items

1. [Ground] Aircraft set up from cold and performance calculation.
2. [Taxi] Engine start and taxi out — CM1.
3. [Take Off] Normal take-off with crosswind technique.
4. [Climb/Cruise] Climb FL200.
5. [Climb/Cruise] UPRT — Normal Law: demonstrate pitch and roll protections.
6. [Climb/Cruise] UPRT — Normal Law: low-speed protections.
7. [Climb/Cruise] UPRT — Normal Law: high-speed protections.
8. [Climb/Cruise] UPRT — Alternate Law: low-speed stability.
9. [Climb/Cruise] UPRT — Alternate Law: high-speed stability.
10. [Climb/Cruise] Upset Recovery — Nose High actions / Nose Low actions.
11. [Climb/Cruise] Demonstrate speedbrake use and effect on VLS.
12. [Climb/Cruise] TCAS event.
13. [Approach] Procedural VOR approach with autopilot.
14. [Approach] Go-around.
15. [Approach] Procedural VOR approach with autopilot.
16. [Landing] Crosswind landing.
17. [Take Off] Reposition for normal crosswind take-off; demonstrate Alpha Lock Protection. (reposition)
18. [Climb/Cruise] Repeat Items 5–16 for CM2. (reposition)

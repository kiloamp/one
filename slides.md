# Regulatory Baseline

- Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/781 amends Regulation (EU) No 1178/2011 and Regulation (EU) No 965/2012.
- It addresses FSTD requirements and the use of FSTDs for pilot training, testing and checking.
- EASA states the new FCS framework and CS-FSTD Issue 1 become applicable from 30 April 2028.
- Selected TRI-related provisions apply earlier from 30 April 2026.

Sources:
[Regulation 2026/781](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/regulations/commission-implementing-regulation-eu-2026781) ·
[EASA FSTD implementation](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/aircrew-and-medical/flight-simulation-training-devices-fstd)

# FSTD Capability Signature

- FCS describes FSTD capabilities, features and fidelity levels.
- Device evidence remains central: qualification certificate, ESL and FCS or assigned FCS.
- CS-FSTD Issue 1 provides the new capability-based certification specification framework.
- The ATO uses device evidence to decide suitability for the approved training programme.

Sources:
[ED Decision 2026/008/R](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/agency-decisions/ed-decision-2026008r) ·
[CS-FSTD Issue 1](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/document-library/certification-specifications/cs-fstd-issue-1)

# Task-to-Tool

- EASA describes task-to-tool as optional for aeroplane and helicopter type rating training and operator recurrent training.
- The method starts with the training task and objective.
- The organisation identifies required capability and fidelity for that task.
- The selected FSTD is checked against those requirements before assigning credit.

Do not treat task-to-tool as mandatory just because the FCS framework exists.

Source:
[EASA FSTD implementation](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/aircrew-and-medical/flight-simulation-training-devices-fstd)

# ATO Impact Areas

- Approved training programmes
- Training Manual / Operations Manual
- FSTD selection procedure
- Instructor briefing and grading procedure
- Compliance monitoring
- Management of change
- Records supporting training credit

---

<!-- .slide: id="evidence" -->

# Evidence File

For each training task, keep enough evidence to show:

1. Training task and objective
2. Required capability and fidelity
3. FSTD certificate, ESL and FCS evidence
4. Suitability decision
5. Limitations or mitigations
6. Instructor record and training credit
